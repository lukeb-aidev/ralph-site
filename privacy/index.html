Your information

Privacy, in plain English.

What Ralph remembers, what online services receive, and the choices you control.

Updated 19 October 2026

Ralph is an iPhone app in development. This policy describes the current app and this website.

What Ralph remembers

Ralph stores your requests, saved plans, selected suggestions, action outcomes, interests, goals and deliberate feedback locally on your iPhone. It also keeps limited local records to avoid repeating suggestions and temporary caches to reuse recent results.

Plans and preferences remain until you change or delete them. Feedback can influence suggestions for up to 90 days and may remain visible until forgotten or replaced. Ralph does not retain AI conversations or model transcripts. Opening something does not automatically mean you liked it.

Searches and other services

Apple’s services receive the information needed to find content and places, such as search terms, your catalogue region and requested item identifiers. A search may use an interest, goal subject or correction you supplied. An explicit nearby-place request sends a fresh location to Apple Maps.

Create can retrieve public artwork and images from The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Those requests use public collection and artwork identifiers, without your saved interests or private context.

These providers receive normal network information when your device connects to them. Their own privacy policies apply. Opening another app, website or sharing destination also brings you under that service’s policies.

Apple’s privacy policy · The Met’s privacy policy

Apple Intelligence

Ralph uses Apple’s on-device models to interpret requests and help choose suggestions. Ralph has no third-party AI backend.

Image Playground is a separate Apple interface. On iOS 27, Apple uses Private Cloud Compute to generate images. Ralph explains this before you continue and does not pass your saved profile or private Ralph context into that interface. Images you accept are stored locally in Ralph; sharing or saving them elsewhere is your choice.

Optional access

Ralph requests permissions when they are relevant to something you choose to do. Where access is already authorised, supported journeys may use temporary Calendar availability, recent workouts or current weather. Health access is read-only. Optional recent Music access lasts for the current app session.

Health and Calendar observations do not become public search terms, saved preferences or exported plans. Ralph does not read your Messages, browsing activity or other apps’ private histories. Contacts uses Apple’s picker; a chosen phone number or email may remain in the plan you reviewed.

Your controls

In Settings → Taste & shared items, you can inspect, correct or forget feedback, disconnect recent Music and control shared captures.

In Settings → Privacy & local data, you can export plans and preferences or delete Ralph’s saved plans and preferences. The screen explains what is removed; some optional setup information has separate controls. Deletion does not remove items already created in Apple’s apps or copies you previously exported or shared. You can change system permissions in iPhone Settings.

This website

This website has no authored analytics, advertising trackers, forms or client-side scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Links to other services follow those services’ privacy policies.

If you contact support through GitHub, your report and profile information are handled by GitHub and the report is public. Do not include private data in that report.

GitHub Pages hosting information · GitHub’s privacy statement

Questions

Use the contact option on Ralph’s Support page. Keep public reports general and leave out private requests, Health information, contact details and exported files.

Your choices stay yours.

Review your saved preferences and feedback. Grant permissions when you need them. Choose what to share and where it goes.
