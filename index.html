Where will you go next?

Ralph is being built around three simple ways to find something worthwhile.

01

Discover

Find something new to read, watch or listen to. Explore real titles and their source details, then follow the link to the selected content.

02

Act

Turn a clear intention into a useful next step. Supported tools help you prepare and review an action before it moves into an Apple app or system interface.

03

Create

Make space for a new idea. Explore creative apps and supported ways to begin, with the destination and any prerequisites explained along the way.

iPhone and iOS

Ralph’s current minimum deployment version is iOS 26.1. iOS 27 is the primary testing target. Final device compatibility will be confirmed with the released build.

Personal AI suggestions

These require an iPhone 15 Pro or iPhone 15 Pro Max, an iPhone 16 model or later, or iPhone Air. Apple Intelligence must be enabled in a supported language and region, with its on-device model ready.
