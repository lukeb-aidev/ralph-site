Ralph support

A little help,
when you need it.

Questions, a choice that missed the mark, or something that did not open as expected? Here is where to start.

Ralph is in development. This guide supports the current development preview.

When a suggestion is not right

Use Tell Ralph to say whether it was worthwhile, already familiar, the wrong subject or the wrong level. Use Not now when the timing is wrong. You can inspect and forget feedback in Settings → Taste & shared items.

When suggestions are unavailable

Personal suggestions need Apple Intelligence to be enabled and its on-device model ready in a supported language and region. If Ralph offers Try again, you can retry. You can also use Make a request for a specific destination or open a supported tool through Your tools.

Online searches need an internet connection. Some destinations need their own app, account, purchase, subscription or permission. Ralph explains relevant requirements before you continue.

When another app opens

Check that the named item or expected interface appeared. An opening does not prove that a message was sent, a purchase was made, or work was completed. Return to Ralph and use the available outcome or recovery controls to report what happened.

Saved & unfinished keeps the selected request available so you can come back to it.

Your data and permissions

Use Settings → Privacy & local data to export or delete Ralph’s saved plans and preferences. Read the explanation before deleting; some setup information has separate controls. You do not need to delete data or reinstall the app as a first troubleshooting step.

You can change Ralph’s permissions in iPhone Settings. See the Privacy page for what Ralph keeps, what leaves your phone and your controls.

What to include in a report

  • Your iPhone model, iOS version and Ralph version.
  • The steps you took and what you expected to happen.
  • What happened instead, including any brief error message.

Only share a screenshot after checking it for personal information. Do not post private requests, Health information, contact details, passwords or exported data in a public report.

Contact Lukas Bower

For questions, feedback or technical help, open a support request on Ralph’s public website repository. A GitHub account is required.

Open a support request

Reports on GitHub are public. Keep your first message general and leave out personal information.

iPhone requirements

The current build requires iOS 26.1 or later. iOS 27 is the primary testing target; final release compatibility is still being verified.

Personal AI suggestions need iPhone 15 Pro or Pro Max, an iPhone 16 model or later, or iPhone Air, with Apple Intelligence available and ready.
